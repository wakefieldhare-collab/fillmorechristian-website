Privacy

How we handle your information

Facebook sermon posts

Fillmore Christian Church uses a small Facebook app, FCC Sermon Posts, for one purpose: to post a link to each week's sermon on the church's own Facebook Page. The app does not collect, store or share information about visitors to our website or about Facebook users.

This website

This website shares information about our church, our worship and our sermons. It does not ask you to create an account. If you write to us using the contact information on this site, we use what you send only to reply to you.

Your information

To ask about any information we hold, or to ask us to remove it, email church@fillmorechristian.org or write to Fillmore Christian Church, 310 N. Florence Street, Fillmore, MO 64449. We will respond and remove the information you ask us to remove.
